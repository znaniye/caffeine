Model

Caffeine in the body: one-compartment pharmacokinetics with first-order absorption and elimination (Bateman function). Cups add up linearly.

A(t) = Σ D · ka/(ka−ke) · (e^(−ke·Δt) − e^(−ka·Δt)) ke = ln2 / t½ ka = 2.7 /h → peak ≈ 1 h after a cup

Faint halo above the curve: where you would be with a 40% longer half-life.

Half-life

Adults range from about 1.5 to 9.5 h (CYP1A2 genetics, liver function, medication).

Sleep line

The message at the bottom asks: is there room for one more cup? It finds the latest time for it that keeps you under the line from bedtime on, counting caffeine still being absorbed. Over the line, it says by when your last cup should have been.
